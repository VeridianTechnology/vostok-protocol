VOSTOK — interactive marble face study

Base geometry: Infinite, 3D Head Scan by Lee Perry-Smith / Infinite-Realities.
Source: https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/dev/examples/models/gltf/LeePerrySmith
Based on a work at www.triplegangers.com.
License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported.
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

Adaptations for this study: softened surface, white marble material, nine
independent regional morph targets, sculpted eye details, and green region masks.
The eyelid relief uses the pre-existing MaleHead project asset recovered from
public/head/malehead.glb in project commit c8970fd (March 2026), baked to a small
depth field. Only the eye area is transferred to the base scan.
This is an artistic visualization, not a scan of the people in the reference
photographs, a clinical simulation, or evidence of a treatment outcome.
